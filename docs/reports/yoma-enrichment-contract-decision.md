# Yoma enrichment contract decision

Final contract decisions for the enrichment fields implicated by the merged
tail-enrichment audit (`docs/reports/yoma-tail-enrichment-audit.md`, PR #476).

This document is the authority for what each field means. It records the
decision, the alternative that was rejected, the evidence, the transition
plan, the validator, the owning task type, and the exact point at which
corpus edits become authorized.

**No corpus content is changed by the PR that introduces this document.**

## 1. display.hint

**Canonical meaning:** a guiding question to hold in mind while reading the sugya.

Rules: it is a question, not a descriptive paragraph; a non-empty value is a
complete natural question; it must not end in an ellipsis or a truncated
word; it ALWAYS ends with a question mark (this is exact and unconditional --
there is no narrow exception mechanism); it must be independently supported
by the declared source range; it may share a learning angle with
`learning.learnerQuestion` but must not be a byte-identical duplicate of it
without a documented reason.

**Rejected alternative:** redefining `hint` as a descriptive paragraph to
match the 77a-88a generation. Rejected because the descriptive form is
exactly the defect the audit traced to a single pre-squash generation, the
question form is the pre-77a convention across the rest of the corpus, and
`app.jsx` presents `hint` as a prompt to hold while reading.

**Evidence:** the audit's provenance section. 373 sugyot are recorded under
`hint_not_a_question`. An additional 9 trailing-ellipsis hints are recorded
separately under `hint_trailing_ellipsis`, so 382 sugyot fail the overall
final-question-form requirement represented by those two mutually exclusive
validator categories, concentrated in the audited tail.

## 2. finalRuling

**Canonical meaning:** the concise halachic bottom line reached by the sugya.

Rules: independent of `display.hint`; never generated by copying or
truncating it; always a string; empty when the sugya is aggadic, narrative,
purely analytical, or unresolved; a non-empty value states only a ruling
actually reached inside the declared source range; a non-empty value ALWAYS
ends with terminal punctuation: a period, question mark, or exclamation
point, optionally followed by EXACTLY ONE closing quote/apostrophe/
parenthesis/bracket (`."`, `.'`, `.)`, `.]`, `?"`, `!"`, and so on) -- the
closer is legal only when it immediately follows one of those three marks. A
bare colon, bare semicolon, bare quote, bare apostrophe, bare parenthesis, or
bare bracket is never a legal ending on its own, and a closer preceded by
anything other than `.`/`!`/`?` (e.g. `word:"`, `word,"`) is rejected the
same way. Implemented exactly by `SENTENCE_END = re.compile(r"[.!?][\"'”\)\]]?$")`
in `scripts/validate_enrichment_contracts.py`. This is the exact, explicit,
unconditional rule, and missing terminal punctuation is itself the
mechanical truncation signal, not a length-based heuristic standing in for
one; never ends in an unexplained ellipsis. **Exact non-empty equality with
`display.hint` is invalid. A prefix copy of `display.hint` is invalid.**

**Rejected alternative:** treating `finalRuling` as a legitimate summary of
`hint`, which would have retrospectively blessed the 29 exact copies.
Rejected because it collapses two distinct fields, and because at 76b and
earlier `finalRuling` is demonstrably an independent halachic statement while
`hint` is a question.

**Supersession:** the 29 `FR_EXACT_COPY_OF_HINT` records carry
`mechanicalDisposition: STRUCTURAL_OR_SCHEMA_DECISION` in the merged audit
because the contract was open at audit time. That decision is now made and
those records are ordinary repairable defects. **The historical audit
dispositions are deliberately not rewritten**; the supersession is recorded
here and in the repair queue.

## 3. requiresUnderstanding, and the new prerequisiteKnowledge

`requiresUnderstanding` keeps its canonical purpose: **stable sugya ids only**,
every id must resolve, no prose is legal, and ids are never invented from prose.

New optional canonical field **`prerequisiteKnowledge: string[]`** holds short
prose descriptions of background knowledge needed before the sugya. Each value
is concise and meaningful; no placeholder boilerplate; no duplicates within a
sugya; never a sugya id.

**Mechanically enforceable vs semantic:** `validate_enrichment_contracts.py`
enforces shape only -- the field is a list when present, every element is a
nonblank string, no exact-duplicate values within a sugya, and no value is
shaped like a sugya id. "Concise and meaningful" and "no placeholder
boilerplate" are prose-quality judgments the validator does not and cannot
make; those remain a human/semantic-review responsibility, never claimed as
validator-proven.

**Rejected alternative:** redefining `requiresUnderstanding` as prose because
938 of 981 live values are prose. Rejected because 43 values are genuine
resolving ids, the field is consumed for learning-path ordering, and prose
cannot serve that purpose. Both needs are real, so both fields exist.

### Later migration design (not performed here)

Owner: `enrichment-schema-migration`.

1. Preflight inventory of every `requiresUnderstanding` value, classified id vs prose.
2. Move prose values to `prerequisiteKnowledge`, **preserving exact wording**.
3. Leave the genuine resolving ids in `requiresUnderstanding`.
4. Never fabricate a relationship, and never derive an id from prose.
5. Emit a full before/after inventory per daf.
6. `requiresUnderstanding_prose` must strictly decrease; no other rule may rise.

**DATA_SCHEMA_VERSION:** not bumped by this tooling PR, because no committed
runtime data shape changes here. It **must** be bumped in the first migration
PR that writes `prerequisiteKnowledge` into `modules/yoma/learning_data.js`,
since that is when the sugya object shape actually changes. `DATA_VERSION` is
the data-layer version and moves with that same migration.

## 4. concepts

**Disposition: delete the entire legacy field.** `concepts` is already marked
`removed` and no consumer reads it. Its legacy `halachic` / `narrative` /
`theological` object members are **not repaired**, and **no new active
contract is created** for that shape. `conceptRefs` is a different reserved
field and is unaffected.

**Rejected alternative:** defining a contract for the object shape and
repairing the 33 semantically wrong records. Rejected because it would give a
removed field a new life, and because the debt is corpus-wide (492 sugyot),
not limited to the 33 the audit happened to examine.

Deletion is **corpus-wide and mechanical**, owned by `legacy-concepts-purge`,
and must prove: only `sugyot[*].concepts` is deleted; no other field changes;
no consumer reads the field; generated output remains fresh; and the number of
deleted fields equals a deterministic preflight inventory.

## 5. topicTags

**Canonical meaning unchanged:** normalized ASCII topic slugs. Lowercase,
hyphen-separated, stable enough for search and semantic navigation, no
periods, apostrophes, spaces or display punctuation, no duplicate slug within
a sugya, and no blind conversion where normalization would create an
ambiguous or misleading slug.

**Rejected alternative:** redefining `topicTags` as human-readable display
labels because legacy data drifted that way (428 sugyot carry invalid slugs).
Rejected: drift is not a contract. **No separate display-label field is added**,
because no consumer requirement for one has been demonstrated - `app.jsx` does
not read `topicTags` at all today.

Migration is deliberately deferred and must be **curated**, because
normalization can collide (for example `R. Meir` and `R-Meir` both normalize
to `r-meir`). The validator reports duplicates and collisions so a later
curated pass can resolve them.

## 6. visualizableElements

**Canonical shape:**

```
{ item: string, type?: string, label?: string, role?: string, priority?: number }
```

`item` is required and is the usable description. `type` is the semantic
category when known. `label` is an optional short display label distinct from
the fuller `item` text. `role` and `priority` are optional because most legacy
elements lack them and they must never be fabricated.

**Rejected alternative:** keeping the old `{ item, role, priority }` contract
with `role` required. Rejected as provably lossy: it cannot represent `type`
(631 elements), `label` (144), or the 101 elements carrying **both**
`description` and `label`, and it would have forced `role` to be invented for
the 648 elements that lack it.

### Lossless mapping proof over all 781 live elements

| Legacy shape | Count | Canonical result |
|---|---|---|
| `item+priority+role` | 49 | unchanged |
| `name+type` | 208 | `item` <- name, `type` |
| `description+type` | 120 | `item` <- description, `type` |
| `description+label+type` | 101 | `item` <- description, `label` kept, `type` |
| bare string | 101 | `{ item: <string> }` |
| `description+priority+role+type` | 84 | `item` <- description, `role`, `priority`, `type` |
| `desc+type` | 75 | `item` <- desc, `type` |
| `label+type` | 43 | `item` <- label, `type` (no separate label, nothing lost) |

Every legacy key maps onto a canonical key with no discarded information.
Legacy keys `name`, `description` and `desc` are transitional inputs only and
must not survive migration.

## Validators

`scripts/validate_enrichment_contracts.py` (generic, `--module`) enforces all
of the above as a **baseline-and-ratchet** gate: current debt is enumerated,
counted and fingerprinted in the module-specific baseline
`scripts/baselines/<module>_enrichment_contract_debt.json` (for Yoma, exactly
`scripts/baselines/yoma_enrichment_contract_debt.json`; a module may never
reuse another module's baseline file, and `verify_baseline_integrity` rejects
a baseline whose stored `module` field disagrees with `--module`); a new
violating sugya id fails even if totals drop; a rising count fails; a falling
count passes and prints the delta; `--targets` (optionally scoped further with
`--rules`) enforces target-clean so a repair PR proves it left every field it
touched compliant. Deleting or weakening a rule is itself a failure, and
rewriting the baseline requires `--update-baseline` in a reviewed docs-tooling
change.

**Registered rules.** `RULES` in `scripts/validate_enrichment_contracts.py`
registers **28 rule ids**, every one of them present in `collect_violations`'s
return value even at zero occurrences (this is what lets a baselined rule
reach zero and still be told apart from a rule quietly deleted or renamed out
of the code): `hint_not_string`, `hint_trailing_ellipsis`,
`hint_not_a_question`, `finalRuling_not_string`,
`finalRuling_trailing_ellipsis`, `finalRuling_unterminated`,
`finalRuling_equals_hint`, `finalRuling_prefix_of_hint`,
`requiresUnderstanding_not_list`, `requiresUnderstanding_prose`,
`requiresUnderstanding_unresolved_id`, `requiresUnderstanding_self_reference`,
`prerequisiteKnowledge_not_list`, `prerequisiteKnowledge_blank`,
`prerequisiteKnowledge_contains_sugya_id`, `prerequisiteKnowledge_duplicate`,
`topicTags_not_list`, `topicTags_invalid_slug`, `topicTags_duplicate`,
`visualizableElements_not_list`, `visualizableElements_bare_value`,
`visualizableElements_missing_item`, `visualizableElements_legacy_key`,
`visualizableElements_unknown_key`, `visualizableElements_field_not_string`,
`visualizableElements_priority_not_numeric`, `legacy_concepts_present`,
`difficulty_invalid_enum`. This count is not asserted from memory: it is the
literal length of `RULES`, re-verified against the committed baseline's own
`ruleRegistry` by `scripts/test_enrichment_contracts.py`.

**Occurrence-level inventory and stable occurrence identity.** Debt is
tracked at OCCURRENCE granularity: `(rule, sugyaId, field path, content
fingerprint)`, not merely per-sugya-id, so an additional occurrence of the
same rule in an already-dirty sugya is a failure even when the sugya was
already non-compliant, and swapping one invalid value for a different
invalid value is a failure even when the occurrence count does not change.
**Array index is deliberately excluded from occurrence identity.** Earlier
revisions of this gate folded the array index into both the field path
(`"topicTags[%d]" % i`) and the fingerprint hash, which meant removing one
invalid array member and letting a later invalid member shift down one index
changed that surviving member's fingerprint -- the ratchet then reported a
false "same-sugya worsening" even though the true violation count fell from N
to N-1. The fix: `fingerprint_occurrence` hashes the INDEX-STRIPPED path
(`strip_array_index`, e.g. `"topicTags[]"`) and the value only; array index
is retained on each occurrence purely as diagnostic/report metadata, never as
identity. `compare_to_baseline` also switched from set semantics to
**multiset (Counter) semantics** on those fingerprints per rule+sugya, so
adding an IDENTICAL extra invalid value (which shares its index-stripped
fingerprint with its sibling) is still caught as growth, while removing one
invalid member and letting the rest shift index is a clean pass.

**Module verification.** `resolve_module`/`baseline_path` derive the
data-file and baseline paths purely from `--module`; `verify_baseline_integrity`
additionally recomputes the baseline's own counts and whole-payload
fingerprint from its occurrence inventory and rejects a hand-edited,
corrupted, wrong-schema-version, or cross-module baseline before trusting it
for comparison.

**Global ratchet plus task-specific rule-scoped target-clean.** The
corpus-wide comparison above (every rule, every sugya) is the unconditional
final word on new debt anywhere; layered on top, `--rules`/`--targets`
narrows target-clean to exactly the rules and sugya ids a given worker task
actually owns (`task_specific_rule_scoped_targets` in
`scripts/worker_pipeline.py`), so unrelated legacy debt on the same or a
different sugya never blocks an otherwise-valid, narrowly-scoped repair.

**Progress lifecycle.** `docs/reports/data/yoma-tail-enrichment-repair-progress.json`
tracks `audited-sugya-enrichment-repair` progress per sugyaId through
`NOT_STARTED -> IN_PROGRESS -> FIXED_PENDING_REVIEW -> APPROVED_PENDING_MERGE
-> COMPLETE` (plus `BLOCKED`, which requires a non-empty `blockerReason`, and
which resolves back to `IN_PROGRESS`). `APPROVED_PENDING_MERGE` and `COMPLETE`
are review-bearing: both require a non-empty `reviewer` and
`independentReviewResult`. Legality is checked across the FULL commit history
from merge-base to head (`check_progress_history` in
`scripts/generate_enrichment_repair_queue.py`), not merely the two endpoints,
so a branch that legally walks every intermediate status is never rejected as
a false "skip." Progress-record changes are scoped to exactly the
manifest's own `auditRecordIds` (`check_progress_scope`); every other record
must stay byte-identical, and no record may carry an unknown field. Post-merge
completion is DERIVED (`derive_effective_status`) from squash-merge evidence
bound to the exact audit record, not merely from any ancestor commit that
happens to touch some `*.learning.json` file. All of the following must
hold: the stored status is `APPROVED_PENDING_MERGE` with a non-empty
`reviewer` and `independentReviewResult`; the squash commit is an ancestor
of head; the squash commit's own `.worker-manifest.json` (read from that
commit's tree, never the working tree) has `type ==
"audited-sugya-enrichment-repair"`; the sugyaId is named in that manifest's
`auditRecordIds`; the manifest's single target daf matches that sugyaId's
own daf in the repair queue; and the squash commit's diff actually touches
that exact daf's `*.learning.json`. A correctly-manifested repair for a
different sugya, or a same-daf manifest naming a different sugya, or a
correct manifest whose commit never touches the target file, all fail to
derive `COMPLETE`. This removes the need for a second, progress-only PR
just to flip the stored status to `COMPLETE`. See
`docs/reports/yoma-tail-enrichment-repair-plan.md` for the full design.

**Migration-prerequisite enforcement.** Separately from semantic-field
target-clean, `audit_repair_prerequisite_errors` in
`scripts/worker_pipeline.py` blocks `audited-sugya-enrichment-repair`
manifest generation AND preflight until (a) `legacy_concepts_present` is
exactly zero across the whole corpus, and (b) every named record's own
`migrationPrerequisites` (as declared per-sugya in the repair queue) are
clean for that exact sugya. Unrelated ordinary debt elsewhere in the corpus,
or a migration the queue does not declare as a prerequisite for that record,
never blocks an otherwise-valid repair.

Unit tests: `scripts/test_enrichment_contracts.py` covers clean, per-rule
invalid, baseline-holds, new-debt-rejected, count-rose-rejected,
repaired-passes, target-clean-fails, target-clean-passes, finalRuling
terminal-punctuation accepted/rejected endings, and array-index-shift
occurrence-identity stability cases, plus the committed baseline's internal
consistency. `scripts/test_worker_pipeline_integration.py` covers the
record-specific audit-scope binding, `<daf>.summary` normalization,
record-specific progress scope, the full one-PR progress lifecycle including
derived post-merge completion, and migration-prerequisite enforcement, all
against the real worker_pipeline.py subcommands running in a disposable
fixture repository.

## Task types

- `legacy-concepts-purge` - delete-only, mechanical, corpus-wide.
- `enrichment-schema-migration` - deterministic migrations, no semantic rewriting.
- `audited-sugya-enrichment-repair` - semantic repair of audited records, one
  daf per PR, independent review required.

## When corpus edits become authorized

1. **Now:** contracts, validators, baseline, task types, queue. No corpus edits.
2. **Next:** `legacy-concepts-purge` (corpus-wide deletion), then
   `enrichment-schema-migration` for `requiresUnderstanding` prose,
   `visualizableElements` shape, and `difficulty`. Mechanical only.
3. **Then, and only then:** `audited-sugya-enrichment-repair` per the repair
   queue, starting at `yoma-082b-s01`. A semantic repair may begin for a sugya
   once its migrations have landed, because target-clean cannot pass while the
   removed `concepts` field and legacy shapes are still present.
4. **Last:** parent daf summaries once every sugya on the daf is settled, and
   `finalRuling`-only repairs once each underlying `hint` is confirmed.

`topicTags` corpus migration remains deferred pending a curated pass; it is
not a prerequisite for the semantic repairs, but a repaired sugya must leave
its own `topicTags` valid.
